2 · Power

Two adapters, two different power leads. The C-Payne GPU adapter takes a PCIe 6-pin; the KALEA M.2 adapter takes a 4-pin 12 V. Neither is powered from the slot. Count your PSU's spare leads before ordering.

3 · BIOS settings that make this work

Setting (Advanced → PCI Subsystem)ValueWhy
U2_P0_G0 Lanesx16Gives GPU 4 a single full x16 link across both MCIO ports
U2_P0_G2 Lanesx4x4Two x4 drives on one MCIO 8i port
U2_P0_G2Bx4x4 (later)Second adapter when you go to 4 drives
SLOT #_Link SpeedAutoForcing Gen4 costs performance; the menu has no Gen5 entry, which is a UI quirk, not a limit
U2_P0_G0/1/2 Link SpeedAutoSame reason — Auto is what trains Gen5
The menu shows no “Gen5” option. That is true of the slots and the MCIO ports alike — the same limitation on hardware the datasheet calls Gen5. Leave everything on Auto. Do not “fix” it by picking Gen4.

4 · Assembly order

  1. Fit CPU + 12 DIMMs, then the board into the chassis with 8 adjacent bracket positions free.
  2. Seat GPUs in Slot_2, Slot_4, Slot_6. Test-fit first — 40 mm cards leave 0.6 mm between neighbours.
  3. Route the G0 MCIO pair to the CP-0232 adapter, mount it, seat GPU 4, connect its 6-pin.
  4. Route G2A to the KALEA adapter, connect its 4-pin, fit the two M.2 drives.
  5. Power: 24-pin, 2× EPS, 4× 12V-2×6, 6-pin, 4-pin.
  6. BIOS: G0 = x16, G2 = x4x4, all link speeds Auto. Confirm all four GPUs and both drives enumerate.
  7. Create the array: mdadm --create /dev/md0 --level=0 --raid-devices=2 …
  8. Before any tensor-parallel run, set NCCL_P2P_DISABLE=1 and iommu=pt, then test whether direct P2P is stable on current ROCm.
Leave Slot_1 empty. It sits one slot pitch from Slot_2 — 20.3 mm centre-to-centre — so no 40 mm card can go there. It is the board's only Gen4 x16 slot anyway.
