Why this is the middle

  • CPU: 9955WX has the 15 expert workers Strata’s AMD reference config uses, 128 lanes and 8 channels. A 9965WX adds €1,378 and cores the engine does not need here.
  • RAM: 128 GB now matches 128 GB VRAM; 8×64 GB = 512 GB is the documented upgrade. Do not buy 128/256 GB modules.
  • Storage: Gen4 990 PRO ×4 is cooler under the GPUs than Gen5 and enough for the PLE random reads; the 4 TB SATA keeps the archive.
  • Power: 2.2 kW ATX 3.1 with 26% headroom; a 1.6 kW unit is at 102% of the continuous load.
  • Cooling: 360 AIO + 3×140 front + 1×140 rear + 2×140 bottom; four blowers exhaust out the back.
  • Engine: Strata’s layer split is the only one of the two R9700 engines that uses all four cards for one model; Saber is TP=2 only. Its 2 KV heads block a dense TP=4; the 4-rank RFC is in docs/SABER-4X.md.
