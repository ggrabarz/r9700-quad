# Seasonic PRIME PX-2200

<p class="muted"><strong>€489.90</strong> · Alternate DE sale (was €579.90) · 2,200 W ATX 3.1, 80+ Platinum. Used in every build.</p>

## What it is

A 2,200 W ATX 3.1 / PCIe 5.1 fully modular PSU with **2× native 12V-2x6** outputs, **2× 2×8-pin-to-12V-2x6 adapters**, **4× PCIe 8-pin**, 3× EPS, 16× SATA, and a 135 mm fan. It is 210 mm long and **200–240 V only**.

It has the watts for 1,630 W continuous (26% headroom), but **not the PCIe 8-pin count** for four GPUs plus board aux. That is the build’s hidden limit.

## Assessment

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Strengths</span>

- **2,200 W**, 26% headroom over the 1,630 W continuous load.
- **Two native 12V-2x6** cables; no adapter for two GPUs.
- **ATX 3.1 / PCIe 5.1**, 80+ Platinum.
- **Fully modular**, 16 SATA, 3 EPS.
- **Quiet at low load**; 135 mm fan.
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Weaknesses</span>

- **Connector-short for four GPUs + board aux**: the 2 adapters use all 4 PCIe 8-pin, leaving none for the ASUS 2× PCIE_8P or the ASRock 4× 6-pin.
- **200–240 V only**; not for 120 V.
- **210 mm long**; check case clearance (Meshify 2 XL fits 250 mm).
- **€489.90** and a heavy unit.
- **The adapter/cable budget is the real constraint**, not the wattage.
</div>
</div>
