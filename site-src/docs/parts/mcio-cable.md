# MCIO 8i/x4 to M.2 NVMe cable

<p class="muted"><strong>€40.00 estimate</strong> · not a listed price · Gen5 x4 adapter. Used in <a href="../../builds/a-balanced/">A</a>–<a href="../../builds/f-full-speed/">F</a>.</p>

## What it is

A cable/adapter from the ASRock’s **MCIO 8i** header to an M.2 NVMe socket, so a Gen5 M.2 drive can live in the case’s 2.5" bay instead of on the board. Two are used for the 3× Gen5 array (one onboard Gen5 M.2 + two MCIO drives).

The price is an **estimate**, not a listed offer. It is one of the few non-verified lines in the BOM.

## Pros

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Pros</span>

- **Moves the Gen5 drive out of the GPU heat** into a 2.5" bay.
- **Cheap** relative to a Gen5 M.2 slot/carrier.
- **No PLX/bifurcation**; the MCIO port is a direct x4.
- **Two cables cover the 3× Gen5 array.**
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Cons</span>

- **Price is an estimate**; exact cable/connector sourcing is not priced.
- **Signal integrity/length** matters at Gen5; buy a rated cable.
- **No hot-swap**; it is an internal cable.
- **Mounting** the drive in a 2.5" bay needs a bracket/caddy.
</div>
</div>
