## What it is

The **G.Skill T5 Neo F5-6400R3848F64GE8-T5N**: an 8×64 GB DDR5-6400 ECC R-DIMM **octa kit**, CL38-48-48-102 at 1.35 V, AMD EXPO. It is a different bin from the 4×64 CL36 kit, specified for eight DIMMs.

On WRX90, eight DIMMs is **1 DIMM per channel**, so 6400 is the CPU’s native 1DPC topology. The kit gives 512 GB and 409.6 GB/s — the full-speed endgame for DeepSeek UD-Q8 and a large model library.
