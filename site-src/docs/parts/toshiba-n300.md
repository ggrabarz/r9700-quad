# Toshiba N300 8 TB

<p class="muted"><strong>€389.00</strong> · Alternate DE · 8 TB SATA HDD. Used in <a href="../../builds/b-capacity/">B</a> and <a href="../../builds/h-asus-4x-gen5/">H</a> for a full array image.</p>

## What it is

A 7,200 rpm NAS-class 8 TB SATA HDD. It is the image/archive drive for the 6 TB Gen4 array and the 4 TB ASUS array. A 6 TB array has ~5.4 TB usable; an 8 TB drive holds the image. A 4 TB array has ~3.6 TB usable; the 4 TB SATA is too small, the 8 TB HDD fits.

## Pros

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Pros</span>

- **Full image of the 4 TB or 6 TB array** on one drive.
- **Cheap per TB**; cold archive is the right job.
- **SATA**, no NVMe port consumed.
- **NAS-class workload rating.**
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Cons</span>

- **HDD speed**; restore is slow (hours).
- **Noise and vibration** in a desktop case.
- **A RAID 0 image is only as good as the last backup**; automate it.
- **3.5" bay and SATA cable** required.
</div>
</div>
