## What it is

The Samsung **M321R8GA0PB0-CWM**: a 64 GB DDR5-5600 CL46 **2Rx4 ECC RDIMM**. It is the volume, JEDEC, QVL-friendly 64 GB module at the snapshot. Two modules give 128 GB in two channels; eight give 512 GB in eight channels.

It is the value floor, not the fast path. The module is rated 5600 and cannot be overclocked to 6400; the 6400 route needs the Micron single modules or the G.Skill kits.

## Pros

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Pros</span>

- **Cheapest per-GB 64 GB RDIMM** in the snapshot.
- **JEDEC, no EXPO**, QVL-friendly and stable at 5600.
- **2× now, 8× later**: the same part fills the 8×64 path.
- **128 GB is the comfortable IQ3_S floor** with PLE in RAM.
- **Available from 28 Geizhals offers.**
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Cons</span>

- **5600 only**: 89.6 GB/s at 2 DIMMs, 358.4 GB/s at 8; no 6400 upgrade.
- **€39.05/GB is still the 2026 DRAM tax**; the pair is 48% of the value platform.
- **2 DIMMs = 2 channels**, a staging configuration for DeepSeek.
- **€2,499 per module** is a lot for a part that cannot reach 6400.
