# SilverStone XE360-TR5

<p class="muted"><strong>€389.90</strong> · Alternate DE sale (was €462.91) · 360 mm sTR5/SP6 AIO, 350 W class. Used in every WRX90 build.</p>

## What it is

A 360 mm all-in-one liquid cooler with an **sTR5/SP6** mounting kit, sized for the 350 W Threadripper PRO. It is designed to top-mount in the Meshify 2 XL and exhaust the CPU heat away from the GPU intakes.

It does **not** fit the Intel W790/LGA4677 alternative; that platform needs a different cooler.

## Assessment

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Strengths</span>

- **sTR5/SP6 mounting** out of the box; no bracket hunt.
- **350 W class**, matched to the 9955WX.
- **360 mm radiator** fits the Meshify 2 XL top.
- **Top exhaust** keeps CPU heat out of the GPU intakes.
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Weaknesses</span>

- **€389.90**; a big line for cooling.
- **AIO pump** is a wear item in a 24/7 box.
- **360 mm top mount** limits case choices; the Meshify 2 XL is fine.
- **Does not fit LGA4677**; the W790 bundle needs its own cooler.
</div>
</div>
