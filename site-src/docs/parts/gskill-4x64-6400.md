# G.Skill 4×64 GB DDR5-6400 kit

<p class="muted"><strong>€12,135.01</strong> · Proshop DE, 1–2 months · 256 GB, 4 channels, CL36. Used in <a href="../../builds/e-fast-ram/">E</a>.</p>

## What it is

The **G.Skill T5 Neo F5-6400R3644E64GQ4-T5N**: a 4×64 GB DDR5-6400 ECC R-DIMM kit, CL36-44-44-102 at 1.30 V, AMD EXPO, 4 channels. Four DIMMs fill half the WRX90 board; 256 GB and 204.8 GB/s.

It is the cheapest 6400 per GB after the 8×64 octa kit, and the smallest G.Skill kit. Alternate lists €12,269 with 17–25 days; Proshop is €12,135.01 with 1–2 months and a two-kit purchase limit.

## Assessment

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Strengths</span>

- **256 GB and 4 channels** for €12,135.01.
- **€47.40/GB**, cheaper than the 2× Micron pair.
- **204.8 GB/s**, enough for DeepSeek 2.00/2.25 and a large PLE/page cache.
- **Leaves four slots open** for the 8×64 endgame.
- **No MCIO/SSD change**; it drops into build E.
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Weaknesses</span>

- **1–2 months lead time** at Proshop; max 2 per customer.
- **Rank/QVL unverified**: G.Skill does not print 2Rx4/2Rx8.
- **CL36 is a different bin** from the 8×64 CL38 kit; two 4×64 kits are not the 8-DIMM configuration.
- **€12,135.01 is 68.3% of build E’s platform**.
- **The PSU connector issue remains** for the four GPUs.
