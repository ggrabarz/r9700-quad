# Fractal Design Meshify 2 XL

<p class="muted"><strong>€209.90</strong> · Alternate DE · EEB 305×330, 9+3 slots, 250 mm PSU, 480 mm top radiator. Used in every build.</p>

## What it is

A big steel-and-glass tower that fits the EEB WRX90 boards, four 2-slot GPUs, a 360 mm top radiator and a 210 mm PSU. It has 9+3 expansion slots, six 3.5" and two 2.5" bays, and a mesh front for airflow.

The four R9700s are 2-slot blowers; the case’s slot count and depth are what make the stack possible. The fourth card’s second bracket lands in the 8th slot.

## Assessment

<div class="pros-cons" markdown>
<div markdown>
### <span class="good">Strengths</span>

- **Fits EEB 305×330**, four 2-slot GPUs, the 360 AIO and the 210 mm PSU.
- **9+3 slots**; the fourth GPU’s second bracket has a home.
- **Mesh front** and good fan mounts for the 1,630 W heat load.
- **Six 3.5" bays** for the 8 TB HDD and future drives.
- **Cheap for the size** at €209.90.
</div>
<div markdown>
### <span class="bad">Weaknesses</span>

- **Big and heavy**; not a desk case.
- **Noise** with four blowers; plan a utility room/rack.
- **Only two 2.5" bays**; the MCIO drives need mounting creativity.
- **Airflow needs setup**: 3 front, 1 rear, 2 bottom for the M.2/GPU zone.
</div>
</div>
