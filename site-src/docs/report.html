The G.Skill 8×64 octa kit is G.Skill’s 8-DIMM 6400 bin at the notify-only €23,094; Proshop can order it at €27,666.06, so eight Micron singles are cheaper if you need 512 GB now. Two 4×64 kits cost €24,270.02 and are not guaranteed at 6400. 8 DIMMs is 1 DIMM per channel on WRX90, so 6400 is the native 1DPC topology.

Why A is the middle

  • Board: ASRock WRX90 WS EVO — proper WRX90 for the 9955WX, IPMI/AST2600, 6 NVMe ports, €808.05. Premium: ASUS WRX90E-SAGE SE, €1,288 — 4× Gen5 M.2, 2× SlimSAS Gen4, 32+3+3+3 VRM, dual-PSU; +€411.95 over A with 3× Gen5 M.2 and no MCIO. The TRX50 AI TOP is a hybrid without IPMI; the WRX90 AI TOP is announced but unpriced.
  • CPU: 9955WX — 16C, 128 lanes, 8 channels, 350 W; the 15 expert workers Strata’s AMD reference config uses.
  • Storage: 3× Gen5 at Gen4 price, all-Gen5, 3 TB, 5.6M IOPS; 3 TB fits a full image on the 4 TB SATA. C is €263.68 more for +1 TB but slower; B is +€1,286.47 for +3 TB and an 8 TB archive.
  • Power: 2,200 W with 26% headroom; a 1,600 W unit is at 102% of the continuous load.
  • RAM: 2×64 GB DDR5-5600 is the value baseline (€4,998); the 2× start at 6400 is 2× Micron MTC40F2046S1RC64BH1 (€6,559.94, JEDEC 2Rx4), and the cheaper-per-GB G.Skill kits are 4×64 (€12,135.01) or 8×64 (€23,094 notify-only; Proshop €27,666.06).
  • Model: 128 GB VRAM holds the 79 GB IQ3_S and 8× 262K streams (21.1 GB KV), with ~6.5 GB left on the main card.
  • Reference: 4× R9700 on ASUS WRX90 Sage, 9955WX, 128 GB DDR5-6400, Radiance TP4 — 236 tok/s single, 1,190 tok/s at 16 streams; user report, not a Strata number.
